TerraWalk is a gamified territory capture fitness app.
Users claim 1km² grid cells by walking/running through them.
Cells can be stolen by other users. The goal is to dominate
the most territory on a global leaderboard.

Prioritise: real-time responsiveness, mobile UX, geospatial accuracy.
Never sacrifice map performance for feature richness.
